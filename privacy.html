Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy applies to the Telangana State Calender app (com.telangana.calender).

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Internet use

The app connects to the internet only to download public holiday data from telangana.gov.in. No information about you or your device is sent with these requests beyond what is technically required to make a standard web request.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.
